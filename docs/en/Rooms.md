The **Rooms** tab is the main organising tool in ESPSomfy-RTS. It lets you split your motors into zones or themes so the interface is easy to navigate.

<img width="100%" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/443274d6-7236-4e3d-a148-0f7ef4d2a97e" />

### Why organise your installation?

If you have a lot of shades, showing them all on one page soon gets confusing. Creating rooms has several advantages:

* **A clearer interface**: a room selection bar appears on the home screen. Choose "Kitchen" and only the shades in that area are shown.
* **Shorter names**: motor names are limited to **20 characters**.
  * *Example*: by assigning a motor to the `Kitchen` room, you can simply name it `Left` instead of `Kitchen Shade Left`.
* **Smart filtering**: groups (central commands) can also be assigned to specific rooms to keep things simple.

### Technical details

* **Capacity**: you can create up to **14 rooms**.
* **The home room**: any shade not assigned to a specific room goes into the main room, called **Home**, by default.
* **Flexibility**: each room name has its own **20-character** limit.


### How to set up your rooms

1. Go to the **Somfy** > **Rooms** tab.

   <img width="800" alt="Rooms tab" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/9c047daa-521b-40a2-89e6-f289b31378b3" />

2. Click the `ADD ROOM` button, enter the name you want, then confirm by clicking `ADD ROOM` again.

   <img width="800" alt="Adding a room" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/227a570c-aed5-4636-b3a6-43cd3d65dbc0" />

3. Once created, these rooms are available in the drop-down menus when you set up your **Shades** or **Groups**.


> [!NOTE]
> Once the room is created, the `ADD ROOM` button becomes `SAVE ROOM`. This lets you change the name at any time. When you have finished editing, your new room appears in the list.

> [!TIP]
> **Organising tip:** you can easily change the order your rooms are shown in by **dragging and dropping** them directly in the list.
